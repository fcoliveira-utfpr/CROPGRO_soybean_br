How to use these cultivars

    
The files SBGRO048_BR.CUL and SBGRO048_BR.ECO work in any DSSAT 4.8 installation. Codes BRG050 to BRG095 are generic Brazilian cultivars for RMG 5.0 to 9.5; codes BR0001 to BR0224 are the cultivars of the grid points, with the median effective RMG of the harvests 2021–2024. 

    
A notebook in the repository runs DSSAT with these cultivars for any location. It was made for people with little or no programming experience: it runs in the browser with Google Colab, with nothing to install. Set the location, seasons and sowing dates in one cell and run all; the notebook downloads the weather from NASA/POWER, runs DSSAT and plots the harvest date and the water limitation for each sowing date and maturity group, to support planning.

    
Phenology was calibrated with a Bayesian model against field experiments, multi-site trials and cultivar catalogues. Growth coefficients are those of BRS 284 (Battisti and Sentelhas, 2019), so simulated yields serve to compare sowing dates, maturity groups and seasons, not to forecast absolute yield. Each cultivar represents the mix grown in a 1° cell. In the South, the effective RMG is higher than that of recommended cultivars, so use those values with care.
